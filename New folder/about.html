About Aurielle Solutions

Dedicated to supporting individuals and families on their path to stable housing

Our Mission

At Aurielle Solutions, we believe everyone deserves a safe, stable place to call home. Our mission is to support individuals and families in Connecticut as they navigate the challenges of housing instability through compassionate guidance, practical resources, and comprehensive support services.

Our Approach

We understand that housing challenges affect each person differently. That's why we take a personalized, trauma-informed approach to every individual we serve. We meet people where they are, respect their autonomy, and work collaboratively to identify solutions that fit their unique circumstances and goals.

Our Values

Dignity & Respect

Every person we serve is treated with the dignity and respect they deserve, regardless of their circumstances.

Empowerment

We believe in empowering individuals to make their own decisions and build the skills they need for long-term success.

Compassion

We approach every interaction with empathy, understanding, and genuine care for those we serve.

Collaboration

We work closely with community partners, agencies, and stakeholders to provide comprehensive, coordinated support.

Our Commitment

We are committed to being a trusted partner in your journey toward stable housing. Whether you're experiencing homelessness, facing the risk of losing your home, or transitioning between housing situations, we're here to provide the support, resources, and guidance you need.
